About Us

A foundation started by three friends

HUMORA FOUNDATION was founded by three friends who share one humanitarian mission: to stand beside people who are often left behind. What began as a shared commitment has become a foundation dedicated to bringing hope, dignity and practical support to communities in Bangladesh and beyond.

A Little Help, A Brighter Future.

Our Vision

A world where every person — whatever their circumstances — can live with dignity, safety and opportunity.

Our Mission

Bringing hope, dignity and support to underprivileged people, street children, poor families and vulnerable communities.

Our Values

Compassion, honesty, transparency and respect for every person we serve.

How we work

We identify people and communities in genuine need.

We deliver help directly — food, clothing, care and education.

We publish verified activities and financial records.

The people at the heart of our mission

Children, families, elders and volunteers — every life deserves care, dignity and a brighter tomorrow.
