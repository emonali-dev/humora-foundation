About Us

A foundation started by three friends

HUMORA FOUNDATION was founded by three friends who share one humanitarian mission: to stand beside people who are often left behind. What began as a shared commitment has become a foundation dedicated to bringing hope, dignity and practical support to communities in Bangladesh and beyond.

  • We identify people and communities in genuine need.
  • We deliver help directly — food, clothing, care and education.
  • We publish verified activities and financial records.
